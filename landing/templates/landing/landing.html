{# ============ Manifesto ============ #}

An hour shapes your day. Your days shape your weeks. Your weeks shape your months. Your months shape your years. And your years shape your life. You don’t need another opinion about how your life is going. You need a record. Ecosystem shows you exactly where the time goes and what keeps shaping your days.
